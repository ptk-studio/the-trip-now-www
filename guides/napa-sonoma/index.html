Three days

Start in downtown Napa at the Oxbow Public Market and a seated tasting in Carneros, head up the valley to Robert Mondavi Winery, St. Helena's Main Street, Castello di Amorosa and the hot springs of Calistoga, then end on historic Sonoma Plaza and at Jack London's ranch in Glen Ellen.

  • Hand-picked places to stay and eat in every price range
  • Tasting fees and reservations, and how to get around without driving after tasting
