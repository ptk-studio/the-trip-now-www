## 2. Direction for 2027

- **Who it's for:** independent travellers planning a **short trip (2–4 days)** to one place, who want a ready, checked plan rather than a 300-page guidebook or a pile of blog tabs. First NorCal and the US West (where the Owner can fact-read), then well-known short-break cities.
- **What it is:** a small travel brand with three shelves for the same places:
  1. **store products: t-shirts, mugs and bags** of the places (a lead focus in 2027, marketed on their own, not only as a guide add-on);
  2. **travel books on Amazon KDP:** pocket itineraries (lead) **plus travel journals, trip planners, logbooks and activity/colouring books of places**;
  3. **Instagram (a community to grow) and thetripnow.com** as the window that links them.
- **What it isn't:**
  - a full guidebook series competing with Lonely Planet on coverage;
  - a high-volume AI book farm (see §6, R1);
  - a booking or affiliate site in 2027.
- **How it fits PTK Studio:** the first user of the reusable 88eggs `Ebook1` pipeline (approved 2026-11-14), which HoneyStorm and other projects reuse. Books publish on the HoneyStorm KDP account with a thetripnow.com back page (Owner, 2026-11-15).

## 6. Not doing, and risks

**Not doing in 2027**
- Paid ads (Amazon Ads, Meta) or paid promotion: needs the Owner's yes; revisit only if a guide rates ≥ 4.2.
- More than 1 new guide a week, and never more than the KDP daily cap.
- Destinations nobody on the team can fact-read without a reliable source base.
- Other marketplaces, translations, audiobooks, an app.
- Affiliate or booking links in books (a separate proposal, later).

**Risks**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 Readers or Amazon treat us as "AI travel guide" spam: disappointing-content removal or 1-star reviews ([KDP G200672390](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200672390); [Futurism, 2023-08-07](https://futurism.com/the-byte/scam-amazon-ai-generated-travel-guides)) | medium | B1; a truthful AI disclosure; a named brand author; no fake reviews ever; slow cadence |
| R2 Outdated facts (closures, prices) | high over time | "last checked" date, 12-month refresh, unpublish if not refreshed; a reader-report link on the back page |
| R3 Low demand for short paid guides vs free AI | high | test with a 3-title pilot and the 30-day check before scaling (proposal milestone 6) |
| R4 Image rights | low | PD/CC0/CC BY only with a credits page; OSM data with ODbL credit |
| R5 Shared KDP account with HoneyStorm: a strike on one hits both | low–medium | the same quality gate for both; the Owner presses Publish per title |
| R6 Another silent pipeline stall (credits, tokens) | medium | the health-check signals (newest store product date, Instagram count) and an alert after 3 days |

## Quality, delight and satisfaction

**Top pain points**
- Ours: the 2-month stall; a store nobody can find; no links between the parts; the first Big Sur pass "not professional enough" (Owner, 2026-11-14), fixed with real photos, a designed cover and maps.
- Competitors': outdated, thin, unreadable maps, generic AI text (above).

**Delight improvements**
1. A "last checked <date>" stamp and a "report a change" link on every guide.
2. Day maps you can actually read: one per day, numbered stops, large labels.
3. "Did you know?" boxes with sourced history and fun facts per stop (Owner, 2026-11-14).
4. A free printable one-page checklist for each trip on thetripnow.com.
5. Named, confirmed-open places to stay and eat in budget tiers (Owner, 2026-11-14).

**Quality bar**
- Release checks pass (RELEASE-CHECKS.md: EPUBCheck 0 errors, images ≥ 300 dpi, KDP Previewer clean).
- 0 unconfirmed claims at the fact check; every place confirmed open on 2+ sources.
- Image licences recorded (credits page).
- Average Amazon rating ≥ 4.0; below that, pause new guides and fix.

**How satisfaction is measured (monthly)**
- Amazon rating and review text per guide, themes logged in FEEDBACK.md.
- Kindle KENP read-through (pages read per borrow), if KDP Select is ever on.
- Back-page UTM visits to thetripnow.com, and store clicks from guide pages.
- Reader "report a change" messages and how fast we fix them.
