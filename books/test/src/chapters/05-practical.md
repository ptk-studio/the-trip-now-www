# Where to Stay and Getting Around

Three days is short, so where you sleep and how you move matter. This chapter covers the three areas to base yourself, the airport bus, the trip to Hạ Long Bay, and getting around on foot.

## Three Areas to Stay

**The Old Quarter.** It is walkable, lively and noisy at night [F13]. Choose it if you want to step out of your door straight into the action. Light sleepers should pack earplugs.

**Around Hoàn Kiếm Lake.** This area is central and a little calmer [F13]. It suits you if you want to be near the middle of things but still sleep well.

**The French Quarter.** South of the lake, it has broader, quieter streets [F13]. It is the most relaxed of the three, so pick it if you value peace over atmosphere.

Whichever you choose, book a place you can reach easily on foot from your first evening's plans. Note that we don't recommend specific properties here, so compare reviews and locations yourself.

## From Nội Bài Airport to the Old Quarter

The simplest budget option is city bus 86. It costs about 45,000 VND (check before you go) and takes about 45–60 minutes [F5]. It runs to Hanoi Railway Station via the Old Quarter [F5], so you can get off in the heart of the old streets if you're staying there. If you're staying elsewhere, check the route on a map before you board and plan the last stretch on foot or by taxi.

## Getting to Hạ Long

Plan on about 2.5–3 hours door to door by car or shuttle on the expressway [F6]. That is a long stretch in a three-day trip, so set off early and bring water, a snack and something to read or watch. Check pickup arrangements with whoever is taking you, so you know where the vehicle collects you.

## Walking the Old Quarter

The Old Quarter is best explored on foot [F13]. Wear comfortable, broken-in shoes, carry water, and use sunscreen and a hat in sunny weather. Let yourself wander, because the district rewards unplanned detours. Keep your phone map handy so you can find your way back when you do.

Walking is also the best way to soak up the lively atmosphere that makes the area a favourite base [F13]. Take breaks when you need them, and save your energy for the sights on your list.
