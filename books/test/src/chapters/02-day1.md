# Day 1: The Old Quarter and Hoàn Kiếm Lake

Today is about slowing down and letting Hanoi come to you. Wear comfortable shoes, carry water, and expect to wander more than you plan.

## Morning: The Trade Streets

Start in the Old Quarter on foot, with no fixed route. Many street names here begin with "Hàng", meaning "goods", after the trade once sold there. [F10]

Look for a few of them as you go:

- Hàng Bạc, for silver [F10]
- Hàng Gai, for silk and hemp [F10]
- Hàng Mã, for paper goods [F10]

You may hear the area called the "36 streets". That is a traditional name, and there are many more streets than that. [F10] So don't try to tick them all off. Turn down whichever lane looks interesting, and use the street signs to enjoy the old trade names as you pass.

## Late Morning: The Lake and Ngọc Sơn Temple

From the Old Quarter, walk to Hoàn Kiếm Lake and take a slow lap of the shoreline. Shade and a bench are easy to find, and it makes a good pause after the busy streets.

Ngọc Sơn Temple sits on the lake. A ticket costs about 30,000–50,000 VND, so check before you go. [F2] If you'd rather save your money, you can view the red Thê Húc Bridge from the shore for free. [F2] Either way, it's a good place for photographs, so bring your camera or phone.

## Midday and Afternoon: Eat by Dish Name

Hanoi rewards eating small and often. Rather than hunting for one famous address, look for a busy, friendly spot serving the dish you want, and ask locals if you're unsure. Four dishes to look for:

- **Phở**: noodle soup. [F11] It's a natural first bowl of the trip.
- **Bún chả**: grilled pork with rice noodles and dipping broth. [F11]
- **Chả cá**: turmeric fish fried with dill. [F11]
- **Egg coffee (cà phê trứng)**: created in Hanoi in 1946. [F11] Have it as a sweet afternoon break.

You don't need to eat all four at lunch. Spread them across the day and share plates if you're travelling with someone.

## Evening: An Easy Stroll

As the light softens, head back toward the lake. The Thê Húc Bridge is free to view from the shore, so it's an easy evening stop even if you skipped the temple ticket earlier. [F2]

From there, wander back into the Old Quarter. The same streets you walked this morning feel different after dark, and the street names make a good game: see how many trades you can spot from the signs. [F10] If you're still hungry, this is a good time for a second bowl of anything from the list above. [F11]

## Tips for the Day

- Pace yourself. A full day on foot is plenty, and a mid-afternoon break is well earned.
- Carry some small cash for snacks and drinks.
- Cross streets slowly and steadily, and let the traffic flow around you.
- Get to bed at a sensible hour: Day 2 starts early, because the Mausoleum opens mornings only. [F3]
