# Eat like a local

Four dishes make a friendly introduction to the table in Hanoi, and three days gives you time to try every one. Think of this chapter as a short checklist you can tick off at your own pace.

## The four dishes

**Phở** is a noodle soup [F11]. It's an easy place to start, and a good one to come back to if you want to compare bowls.

**Bún chả** is grilled pork served with rice noodles and a dipping broth [F11]. It's a dish with a few parts, so enjoy putting each bite together the way you like it.

**Chả cá** is turmeric fish fried with dill [F11]. If you like fish with plenty of character, this one is for you.

**Egg coffee**, or cà phê trứng, was created in Hanoi in 1946 [F11]. Treat it as a little taste of the city's history in a cup.

## Try all four

Spread them across your three days so each one gets your full attention. You might meet one dish on your first day, another on your second, and save the rest for a last-day finish. There's no wrong order, and trying all four is the goal.

## How to order

You don't need to speak Vietnamese to get what you want. Point at the dish on the menu, or ask for it by name: phở, bún chả, chả cá, or cà phê trứng [F11]. Saving the four names in your phone makes it easy to show them or read them out.

Order with a smile, take your time, and enjoy each dish as it arrives.
