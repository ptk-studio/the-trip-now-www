# Day 3: Hạ Long Bay Day Cruise

## An early start

Today you leave Hanoi for the coast, so set your alarm early and pack the night before. The expressway journey takes about 2.5-3 hours door to door by car or shuttle [F6]. Take a light breakfast, a bottle of water and something to do on the road, such as a book, a playlist or a nap.

Wear comfortable, flat-soled shoes, and bring sunscreen, a hat and a light layer, since it can feel cooler on the water. Keep your bag small, because deck space is limited.

## Among the limestone islands

Hạ Long Bay has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994, and it holds more than a thousand limestone islands and islets [F12]. Photos prepare you for the shapes, but not for the scale. Once the boat leaves the harbour, the islands crowd in, overlap and fade into one another.

A day cruise suits a short trip like this one. You get a full day on the water and still sleep in Hanoi tonight. Spend most of it on deck. The light shifts constantly, so keep your camera or phone within reach. Details such as meals, stops and activities differ from boat to boat, so check locally when you book.

## The fee note

Day cruises normally include the bay's sightseeing fee in the ticket price, but ask before you pay [F7]. A clear answer up front avoids surprises at the pier. Also ask what else the ticket covers, so you know whether to carry extra cash for drinks or souvenirs.

## Weather and your spare day

The most comfortable months are October to April [F9]. From July to November there is a typhoon risk, and cruises do not sail on storm days [F9]. If you are travelling in that window, keep a spare day in your plan [F9]. Treat it as insurance rather than a loss. If the weather holds, you can spend it lingering over the Old Quarter or revisiting a favourite spot.

Check the forecast the evening before. If your cruise is cancelled, ask about rebooking and use your spare day.

## The drive back

The return trip takes about 2.5-3 hours, the same as the morning drive [F6]. Expect to arrive tired and happy. Rest on the way, because the sea air and sun tend to catch up with you in the car.

Once you are back in Hanoi, keep the evening simple. A slow stroll and a light meal will do. If you are flying out soon, give yourself a generous margin, and remember that the journey back from the bay takes a while [F6].

## Quick checklist

- Leave early and allow for the road [F6].
- Ask whether the sightseeing fee is in your ticket [F7].
- Check the season and keep a spare day if you are travelling July to November [F9].
- Pack water, sunscreen, a hat and a light layer.
