# Day 2: Temple of Literature, Ba Đình and Water Puppets

Today is a gentle mix of ceremony, calm and a little theatre. Start early, because the first stop only opens in the morning.

## Morning: the Mausoleum area

The Hồ Chí Minh Mausoleum is open mornings only, so make it your first stop. [F3] Entry is free. [F3] Exact opening hours aren't listed here, so check locally on the day.

Two closures can spoil the plan:

- The Mausoleum is closed on Mondays and Fridays, so if your Day 2 falls on one of those days, swap this itinerary with another day. [F3]
- It also closes for about two months each year for maintenance, usually in autumn. [F3] Look up the schedule before you build your trip around it. [F3]

If the Mausoleum is closed, you can still enjoy a slow walk around the Ba Đình area. Wear comfortable shoes and carry water, since the morning is mostly spent on your feet.

## Midday: rest and refuel

Take a break after the morning. Have a relaxed lunch, drink some water and rest your feet. You have a long afternoon and evening ahead.

## Afternoon: the Temple of Literature

The Temple of Literature (Văn Miếu) is open daily, about 8:00–17:00. [F1] The last entry is about 16:30, so don't leave it too late. [F1] An adult ticket costs about 70,000 VND, and it is cash only, so bring notes with you. [F1]

Dress with care. Tank tops and miniskirts are not allowed, so wear something that covers your shoulders and knees. [F1] If you are coming straight from a hot morning, a light shirt and long trousers or a longer skirt will do.

Give yourself an unhurried visit. Courtyards like these reward slow walking, so pause often rather than ticking off sights.

## Evening: Thăng Long Water Puppet Theatre

End the day with a water puppet show at the Thăng Long Water Puppet Theatre. Each show lasts about 50 minutes, which makes it an easy fit after dinner or before a late meal. [F4] There are several shows each afternoon and evening, so you can choose a time that suits your energy. [F4]

Book ahead. Evening shows often sell out from November to March, so reserve a day in advance. [F4] Standard seats start at about 100,000 VND. [F4]

## Budget note

Today's costs are small: the Mausoleum is free [F3], the temple is about 70,000 VND [F1] and standard puppet seats start at about 100,000 VND [F4]. These figures are approximate, so check before you go.

## Day 2 at a glance

1. Morning: Hồ Chí Minh Mausoleum, if it is open. Remember the Monday and Friday closures and the annual maintenance closure. [F3]
2. Midday: lunch and a rest.
3. Afternoon: Temple of Literature, with last entry about 16:30. [F1]
4. Evening: water puppets, booked a day ahead if you are travelling from November to March. [F4]
