# Packing List

Pack light. You will walk a lot, and Day 3 is a long day out on the bay.

## Everyday essentials

- Comfortable, broken-in walking shoes
- A refillable water bottle
- Sunscreen and a hat
- A light rain jacket or compact umbrella
- Phone, charger and a power bank
- A universal plug adapter
- A small daypack for sightseeing
- Any personal medication, plus a basic first-aid kit

## Temple-appropriate clothing

- Shirts with sleeves, not tank tops [F1]
- Trousers, or skirts and shorts that fall below the knee, since miniskirts are not allowed [F1]
- A light scarf or shawl to cover your shoulders if needed

## Cash

- Carry Vietnamese dong in small notes. The Temple of Literature takes cash only, and an adult ticket is about 70,000 VND, so check before you go. [F1]
