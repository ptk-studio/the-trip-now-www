# Napa & Sonoma Wine Country in 3 Days

This guide is for first-time and returning visitors who want a relaxed, well-paced long weekend in wine country. You get three days and plenty of room to linger.

## The 3-day shape

- **Day 1:** Napa and Carneros.
- **Day 2:** Up the valley.
- **Day 3:** Sonoma and Glen Ellen.

## Getting there

Downtown Napa is about 50 miles north of San Francisco: about 1 hour on a weekday and up to about 2 hours on weekends. [N1] The routes, the ferry and the drive times are in Practical Matters.

## When to go

Harvest ("crush") runs from late summer through fall. [N14] Sparkling-wine grapes are often picked from early August, and Cabernet Sauvignon comes last, into October or later. [N14] It is the peak travel season, so book tastings, hotels and dinners well ahead. [N14] In 2026 the crush began early, on July 21. [N14]

Mustard season, when wild yellow mustard blooms between the vines, runs January through March and peaks about mid-February. [N14] Weekdays are quieter than weekends all year. [N14]

Book tastings ahead, especially at weekends. [N4] **The driver doesn't taste** (see Practical Matters), and tasting is for guests 21 and older, so bring ID. [N15][N16]
