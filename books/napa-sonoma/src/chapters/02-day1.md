# Day 1: Napa and Carneros

## Oxbow Public Market

Start in downtown Napa at Oxbow Public Market, at 610 & 644 First St on the Napa River. [N2] It is open daily about 7:30 am–9 pm, though vendors set their own hours. [N2] It closes on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day. [N2] Free lots sit on the north and south sides. [N2]

> **Did you know?** The Oxbow District takes its name from the bend the Napa River makes here. [N2] The market's developer, Steve Carlin, had earlier managed San Francisco's Ferry Building Marketplace. [N2]

## A Carneros tasting

Head south of Napa to Carneros. The Los Carneros wine region has been an official AVA since 1983 and is the only one that crosses both Napa and Sonoma counties. [N3] Its cool, bay-influenced climate suits sparkling wine. [N3]

Domaine Carneros is its best-known château. [N3] It was founded in 1987 by the family behind Champagne Taittinger. [N3] Tastings are seated only and reservations are required. [N3] It is open daily from 10 am, with the last reservation at 5 pm. [N3] Tasting fees aren't published on its visit page, so check before you go. [N3]

Tasting is for guests 21 and older, so bring ID. [N16] Remember: the driver doesn't taste (see Practical Matters). [N15]

> **Did you know?** "Los Carneros" is Spanish for "the rams", after the sheep that once grazed these hills. [N3]

## How tasting works

Most tastings are seated and booked ahead, and walk-in space is limited, especially on weekends and in harvest season. [N4] Typical fees are in Practical Matters.
