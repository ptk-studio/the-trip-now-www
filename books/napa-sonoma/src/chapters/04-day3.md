# Day 3: Sonoma and Glen Ellen

## Sonoma Plaza and the Mission

Start on Sonoma Plaza. General Mariano Vallejo laid out the 8-acre square in 1835. It is the largest of its kind in California and a National Historic Landmark (1961). [N11]

Sonoma State Historic Park covers the Mission, the Sonoma Barracks and Vallejo's sites. It is open daily 10:00-17:00 and costs about $3 per adult. It closes on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day. [N11] Parking is free behind the Barracks. [N11]

Mission San Francisco Solano (1823) was the 21st, last and northernmost of California's missions. It was also the only one founded under Mexican rule. [N11]

Many tasting rooms ring the plaza. Several take walk-ins, but book on weekends. [N11]

The driver doesn't taste. In California it is illegal to drive with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.08% or more, so choose a designated driver, take a tour with a driver, or use Uber/Lyft. [N15] Tasting is for guests 21 and older, so bring ID. [N16]

> **Did you know?** On June 14, 1846, American settlers raised the Bear Flag on this plaza in the Bear Flag Revolt. The bear is still on California's state flag. [N11]

## Jack London State Historic Park, Glen Ellen

Drive a short way from the Plaza to Glen Ellen. [N12] The park is open daily 09:00-17:00 and closed on Christmas. [N12] The House of Happy Walls museum is open 10:00-17:00. [N12] The day-use fee is about $10-15 per vehicle, so check before you go. [N12]

Trails lead through Jack London's Beauty Ranch to the ruins of Wolf House. [N12] Wear comfortable shoes and bring water.

> **Did you know?** Wolf House, the Londons' stone dream home, burned on the night of August 22, 1913, shortly before they were due to move in. Jack London's ashes lie on a nearby knoll under a rock from the house, and Charmian later built the House of Happy Walls. [N12]

## Option: Healdsburg

If you want a longer day, Healdsburg is about 50 minutes north of Sonoma via CA-12 and US-101, in northern Sonoma County. [N13] It is a walkable town around a central plaza laid out in 1857 by Harmon Heald. Tasting rooms, galleries and restaurants ring the plaza. [N13] Plan on a full outing, since the drive adds about 50 minutes each way. [N13]

> **Did you know?** Tiny Healdsburg has two Michelin-starred restaurants in 2026. One holds three stars, and the other earned its first star in 2026. [N13]
