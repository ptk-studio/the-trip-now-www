# Day 2: Up the Valley

Today you head north from Napa toward Calistoga, with a historic winery, a castle and a spa town along the way.

## The two roads up the valley

- Two parallel roads run from Napa to Calistoga, and the valley is about 35 miles from end to end. [N5]
- Highway 29 passes Yountville, Oakville, Rutherford and St. Helena, and it is the busier road. [N5]
- The Silverado Trail on the east side is usually quieter. [N5]
- Cross roads link the two, so you can go up one and come back on the other. [N5]

Tasting is for guests 21 and older, so bring ID. [N16]

## Robert Mondavi Winery, Oakville

- The winery stands in Oakville on Highway 29. It was built in 1966, with an arch and bell tower designed with Cliff May. [N6]
- It reopened on April 20, 2026, after a three-year renovation, for its 60th anniversary. [N6]
- The entry-level Pioneer Tasting costs about $60 per person and takes about 60 minutes. [N6]
- Book ahead if you can. Walk-ins are welcome when there's room. [N6]

> **Did you know?** The estate sits in the To Kalon Vineyard, a name from the ancient Greek for "the highest beauty". [N6]

## St. Helena

- St. Helena has a walkable Main Street halfway up the valley. [N7]
- Charles Krug Winery was founded in the mid-1800s and is one of Napa's oldest wineries. It is California Historical Landmark No. 563. [N7]
- The Mondavi family bought it in 1943. [N7]

> **Did you know?** A burger stand on Main Street serves burgers from the 1949 Taylor's Refresher drive-in stand, and the old Taylor's sign is a town historic landmark. [N7]

## Option: the Napa Valley Wine Train

If you would rather not drive up the valley, the train is one way to do it. [N8]

- It leaves downtown Napa (1275 McKinstry St) on a 36-mile round trip to St. Helena and back, past Yountville, Oakville and Rutherford. [N8]
- The lunch train runs about 3 hours, from about 11:30 am to 2:30 pm. [N8]
- Fares run from about $200 to $800+ depending on the experience and date. Book ahead. [N8]

> **Did you know?** The line was founded in 1864 by Samuel Brannan to carry visitors to his Calistoga hot springs. The train uses restored 1915–1917 Pullman dining cars and has carried wine-country guests since September 1989. [N8]

## Castello di Amorosa, near Calistoga

- This castle winery is built in 14th-century Tuscan style. [N9]
- It is open daily from about 10 am to 5:30 pm, by reservation only. [N9]
- Tastings start at about $40. Guided castle tours with a tasting start at about $55. [N9]

> **Did you know?** Owner Dario Sattui began building in 1994 and opened the castle in April 2007. It has 107 rooms on eight levels. [N9]

## Calistoga

- Calistoga sits at the top of the valley. [N10]
- It is known for hot springs and mud baths at spas along Lincoln Avenue. [N10]
- Book a spa ahead. Prices vary by package. [N10]

> **Did you know?** Sam Brannan opened a hot-springs resort here in the early 1860s. The popular story is that he meant to say "the Saratoga of California" and it came out "the Calistoga of Sarafornia". [N10] Calistoga's Chateau Montelena made the 1973 Chardonnay that beat the top French whites at the "Judgment of Paris" blind tasting on May 24, 1976. [N10]

## Where to stay

- **Bothe-Napa Valley State Park** (budget, $): campsites, restored historic cabins and yurts in a state park between St. Helena and Calistoga. Book on ReserveCalifornia. [STAY-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]
- **Dr. Wilkinson's Backyard Resort & Mineral Springs, Calistoga** (mid, $$): walk to town, with mud baths and mineral pools. [STAY-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]
- **Calistoga Motor Lodge & Spa** (mid, $$): 50 rooms, two hot-spring mineral pools and a spa. [STAY-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]
- **Indian Springs Calistoga** (upper, $$$): California's oldest continuously operating thermal pool and spa. [STAY-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]

- **Gott's Roadside, St. Helena** ($): the original 1949 roadside stand. [EAT-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]
- **Farmstead at Long Meadow Ranch, St. Helena** ($$–$$$): the ranch's own restaurant on Main Street. [EAT-STHELENA-CALISTOGA]
