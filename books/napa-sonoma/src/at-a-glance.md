# At a glance

| Day | Where | Main stops |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Downtown Napa and Carneros | Oxbow Public Market; a seated tasting in Carneros (Domaine Carneros) |
| **2** | Up the valley to Calistoga | Robert Mondavi Winery, Oakville; St. Helena; Castello di Amorosa; Calistoga's spas. Option: the Wine Train |
| **3** | Sonoma and Glen Ellen | Sonoma Plaza and the Mission; Jack London State Historic Park. Option: Healdsburg |

## Drive times (about; traffic varies)

- San Francisco to downtown Napa: about 50 miles, about 1 hour on a weekday, up to about 2 hours on weekends. [N1]
- Napa to Sonoma: about 12–15 miles, about 20–30 minutes. [N1]
- The valley, Napa to Calistoga: about 35 miles end to end, on Highway 29 or the Silverado Trail. [N5]
- Sonoma Plaza to Glen Ellen: a short drive. [N12] Sonoma to Healdsburg: about 50 minutes. [N13]

## Book ahead

- Domaine Carneros: seated tastings, reservations required. [N3]
- Robert Mondavi Winery: booking ahead recommended. [N6]
- Castello di Amorosa: by reservation only. [N9]
- The Wine Train and the Calistoga mud baths. [N8][N10]
- Dinners on weekends and in harvest season. [N16]
