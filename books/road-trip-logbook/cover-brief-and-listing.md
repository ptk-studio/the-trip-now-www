**Description (draft, about 150 words):** Your road trips deserve better than a note on your phone. This glovebox logbook gives each of 25 trips four pages: **Plan** (where, when, who, and what is booked, paid and confirmed), **Route log** (one line a stop, with a wide notes column), **Costs** (fuel with the odometer, then food, tolls, tickets and more, so you know your real miles per gallon and budget) and **Memories** (the best moment, a photo, the tickets, and what to do next time). Inside the back: **US road checklists** for the car, tolls and parks, winter and mountain driving, kids and pets, a packing list, a car check, two pages for tickets and receipts, and notes. **Undated**, so you can start any day. 6 × 9 in, 120 pages, plain black-and-white pages with clean ruling. Prices, hours and road rules change: check before you go.
