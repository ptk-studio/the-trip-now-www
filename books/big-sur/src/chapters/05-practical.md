# Practical Information

## Driving times

San Francisco to Monterey is about 113–120 miles. [B1] Allow about 2–2.5 hours via US-101, CA-156 and CA-1, or about 2.5–3 hours all on Highway 1. Traffic varies. [B1]

## Day-use fees and your shared receipt

- Point Lobos State Natural Reserve: about $10 per car. [B6]
- McWay Falls in Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park: about $10 per car. [B8]
- Pfeiffer Beach: day use about $15. [B9]

Keep your receipt. The same day's McWay Falls receipt also works at Point Lobos, Pfeiffer Big Sur and Andrew Molera. [B8] Plan your stops so one fee covers as many as possible.

## Parking tips

- Point Lobos is day use only. It opens about 08:00 and closes at sunset (about 19:00 in summer). [B6] The lot fills early, so arrive early or park on Highway 1 and walk in. [B6] No vehicles over 21 ft are allowed inside. [B6]
- Pfeiffer Beach is reached by narrow Sycamore Canyon Road, about 2.2 miles long, with no RVs or trailers. [B9] The 65-car lot is full on summer weekends, so go early or on a weekday. [B9]
- McWay Falls is a short overlook walk of about 0.6 mile round trip, so you won't need much time there. [B8]

## Highway 1 closures

Slides and fires close stretches of Highway 1. [B10] In 2026 that meant Regent's Slide early in the year and fire-support closures in August and September. [B10] Always check Caltrans QuickMap (quickmap.dot.ca.gov) before you go. [B10] Check again the morning you drive.

## Cell service and fuel

Cell service in Big Sur is very limited, so download offline maps before you leave. [B11] Gas stations are few and pricey, so fill up in Carmel or Monterey. [B11] Carry water and layers, too.

Prices, hours and road status change, so check before you go.
