# Day 2: Carmel-by-the-Sea and Point Lobos

Today is a slower day. You'll stroll through a storybook village in the morning, then spend the afternoon on the coast just to the south. Wear comfortable shoes and bring a layer.

## Morning in Carmel

Leave the car parked and explore on foot. Houses in Carmel have names instead of street numbers, so you'll be looking for names rather than numbers. [B5]

## The Storybook Cottages

Hugh Comstock built his fairy-tale cottages between 1924 and 1928. [B5] Start with Hansel and Gretel on Torres Street, between 5th and 6th. [B5] Then wander the nearby lanes and see how many more you can spot.

> **Did you know?** Comstock built the first storybook cottage for his wife's handmade dolls. [B5] Clint Eastwood was the town's mayor from 1986 to 1988. [B5]

## Carmel Beach

Finish the morning at Carmel Beach, which welcomes dogs. [B5] Bring yours, or just enjoy watching everyone else's. Layers help here, since coastal mornings can be cool.

## Afternoon at Point Lobos

Point Lobos State Natural Reserve lies just south of Carmel. [B6] It is day use only, and it's open from about 8:00 to sunset, which is about 19:00 in summer. [B6] Day use costs about $10 per car. [B6]

The lot fills early, so arrive early or park on Highway 1 and walk in. [B6] For an afternoon visit, plan on the walk-in option. [B6] No vehicles over 21 ft are allowed inside. [B6] Carry water and keep a layer handy as you explore.

> **Did you know?** The little Whalers Cabin was built in the 1850s by Chinese fishermen. [B6] Portuguese shore whalers used the cove from 1862. [B6] Today the cabin is a small museum. [B6]

## Where to stay

- **Cypress Inn:** mid–upper; in the village; Doris Day's hotel; pet-friendly. [STAY-CARMEL]
- **Mission Ranch:** mid; Clint Eastwood's restored ranch, with sheep in the meadow and views of Point Lobos. [STAY-CARMEL]

## Where to eat

- **Hog's Breath Inn:** founded by Clint Eastwood in 1970; courtyard with fire pits; steak or chowder; $$–$$$. [EAT-CARMEL]
- **Mission Ranch restaurant:** sunset dinner with the meadow and Point Lobos view; piano bar; $$$. [EAT-CARMEL]

Prices, hours and road status change, so check before you go.
