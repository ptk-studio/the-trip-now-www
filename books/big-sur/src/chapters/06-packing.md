# What to Pack

The coast changes quickly, so pack for a few kinds of weather and for long stretches without help.

## Layers for fog and wind

Bring a light base layer, a warm middle layer and a windproof jacket. Summer is the busiest season and has frequent morning fog, so a cold start can turn into a bright afternoon. [B12] September–October is the clearest and quietest time to go, but the wind off the water can still chill you at overlooks. [B12] If you come between November and March, the rainy season, add a waterproof shell. [B12]

## Comfortable walking shoes

You'll be on cliff paths, beach sand and uneven trails. Wear broken-in shoes with good grip, and keep a dry pair in the car.

## Water and snacks

Pack more water than you think you'll need, plus snacks for the road. Stops can be far apart, so having your own food means you can pull over wherever the view is best.

## Maps and power

- **Offline maps:** Cell service in Big Sur is very limited, so download your maps before you leave. [B11]
- **A paper map:** It needs no signal or battery, and it makes a good backup.
- **A phone charger:** Bring a car charger and a small power bank.

## Fuel up first

Gas stations in Big Sur are few and pricey, so fill your tank in Carmel or Monterey. [B11]

Prices, hours and road status change, so check before you go.
