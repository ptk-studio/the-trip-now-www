# Day 1: Monterey

Start your trip with a day that moves from waterfront history to aquarium wonders to a scenic coastal drive. Wear comfortable shoes and bring a layer, since the bay breeze can turn cool.

## Cannery Row in the Morning

Begin on Cannery Row while the day is still fresh. Sardine canning started here in 1902, and the canneries had closed by the mid-1960s. [B2] Wander the street at an easy pace, then walk to the aquarium at the other end.

> **Did you know?** The street was called Ocean View Avenue until 1958, when it was renamed after John Steinbeck's 1945 novel "Cannery Row". [B2]

## Monterey Bay Aquarium

The Monterey Bay Aquarium sits right on Cannery Row. [B3] It opened in 1984 on the old Hovden sardine cannery site. [B3] An adult ticket costs about $65, and you should book ahead. [B3] It is usually open about 10:00–17:00. [B3]

> **Did you know?** The cannery's boiler house and smokestacks were kept. [B3] The Kelp Forest tank is about 28 ft tall and was one of the first to keep a living kelp forest. [B3]

## 17-Mile Drive in the Afternoon

After the aquarium, head for Pebble Beach and 17-Mile Drive. It is a private toll road, and you pay about $12–12.50 per car at the gate. [B4] The road is open sunrise to sunset. [B4] Take your time, pull over where it is safe, and keep your camera handy.

> **Did you know?** The Lone Cypress on its rock above the surf has been the Pebble Beach Company's logo since about 1919. [B4] Photos for yourself are fine, but commercial use of its image isn't. [B4]

## Where to stay

- **HI Monterey Hostel**, 778 Hawthorne St, a block from Cannery Row: budget, with private rooms or dorms. [STAY-MONTEREY]
- **Asilomar Hotel & Conference Grounds**, Pacific Grove: mid-range, with historic lodge buildings in the dunes on a state-park beach. [STAY-MONTEREY]
- **Monterey Plaza Hotel & Spa**, 400 Cannery Row: a splurge, built over the water. [STAY-MONTEREY]

## Where to eat

- **Old Fisherman's Grotto**, on the wharf since 1950: clam chowder in a sourdough bowl, $$. [EAT-MONTEREY]

Prices, hours and road status change, so check before you go.
