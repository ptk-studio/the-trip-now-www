# Lake Tahoe in 3 Days

This guide is for first-time and returning visitors who want three easy days around the lake: beaches and a castle in the south, quiet parks on the West Shore, and an old railroad town in the mountains to finish.

## The 3-day shape

- **Day 1:** South Lake Tahoe and Emerald Bay.
- **Day 2:** the West Shore to Tahoe City.
- **Day 3:** Truckee and the North Shore.

## Getting there

South Lake Tahoe is about 190–200 miles from San Francisco, roughly 3.5–4 hours by car. [T1] The routes, the winter roads and getting around without a car are in Practical Matters.

## The lake

Lake Tahoe is about 1,645 ft deep at its deepest point, the second-deepest lake in the US after Oregon's Crater Lake. [T2] Its surface sits about 6,225 ft above sea level, with about 72 miles of shoreline. [T2]

> **Did you know?** Scientists have measured the lake's clarity with a white disk since 1968: about 102 ft deep that first year, and about 72 ft as the 2022 average. [T2]

## When to go

Summer is the busiest season. [T15] In fall, the aspens of Hope Valley on Highway 88 turn yellow, usually best in early to late October, though it varies by year, so check before you go. [T15] Winter is ski season at Palisades and Heavenly. [T15] Weekdays are quieter all year. [T15]

Prices, hours and seasons change, so check before you go.
