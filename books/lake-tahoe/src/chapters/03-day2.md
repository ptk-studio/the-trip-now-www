# Day 2: The West Shore to Tahoe City

## Sugar Pine Point and the Ehrman Mansion

Head north up the West Shore to Ed Z'berg Sugar Pine Point State Park and the Hellman-Ehrman Mansion, built in 1903. [T7] House tours ran daily from May 23 to September 30 in 2026, on the half hour from 10:30 am to 3:30 pm, about 45 minutes each, first come, first served. [T7] Check the tour price before you go. [T7]

In winter, check the road first; see the winter box in Practical Matters. [T5]

## Tahoe City and the Gatekeeper's Museum

In Tahoe City, the Gatekeeper's Museum stands beside the Lake Tahoe Dam, where the Truckee River leaves the lake, its only outlet. [T8] It's open about April 1 to October 31, Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 4 pm, and by appointment in winter; the Marian Steinbach Indian Basket Museum is part of it. [T8]

> **Did you know?** The museum's first cabin housed the gatekeeper, who set how much water flowed out of the lake. [T8]

## Palisades Tahoe

North of Tahoe City, Olympic Valley is home to Palisades Tahoe. [T12] It was renamed Palisades Tahoe on September 13, 2021, after consultation with local Washoe groups. [T12]

> **Did you know?** The valley hosted the 1960 Winter Olympics, the first Winter Games shown on American television. [T12]
