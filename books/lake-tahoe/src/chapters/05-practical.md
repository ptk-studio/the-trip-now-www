# Practical Matters: Getting There, Getting Around, Winter

## Getting there

South Lake Tahoe is about 190–200 miles from San Francisco, roughly 3.5–4 hours via I-80 and then US-50; Friday traffic adds an hour or more, and chain controls much more. [T1] US-50 is the direct road to the South Shore, mostly one lane each way over Echo Summit. [T1] I-80 goes to Truckee and the North Shore, about 187 miles from San Francisco, a wider road that usually stays open longer in storms. [T1] Check a live map before you leave. [T1]

## Getting around without a car

Two free, on-demand shuttles run around the lake. [T13] TART Connect covers North Lake Tahoe and Truckee (its summer 2026 season ran June 18 to September 7 in Truckee and July 1 to September 7 around North Lake Tahoe), and Lake Link covers South Lake Tahoe and Stateline; book in their apps. [T13] The two don't connect, so check the transit sites for a ride between the shores. [T13]

## Winter box: chains and closures

- **Chains:** Caltrans posts three chain levels, R1, R2 and R3. [T14] In R3 every vehicle needs chains, and 4WD and AWD vehicles must still carry chains in any chain-control area. [T14] The speed limit drops to 25–30 mph when chains are required. [T14]
- **Emerald Bay:** Highway 89 around Emerald Bay closes after heavy snow and avalanches, sometimes for days. [T5]
- **Before you drive:** check Caltrans QuickMap or call 1-800-427-7623. [T14]

Prices, hours and seasons change, so check before you go.
