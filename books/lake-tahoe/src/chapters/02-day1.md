# Day 1: South Lake Tahoe and Emerald Bay

## Stateline and the Heavenly Gondola

Start at Heavenly Village, where the California–Nevada line crosses US-50. [T3] On the Nevada side, Stateline's casino hotels are all a short walk from the village. [T3] The Heavenly Gondola climbs from the village plaza; summer sightseeing ran about 10 am to 5 pm daily in 2025; hours change each year, so check before you go, and see skiheavenly.com for tickets. [T3]

## Emerald Bay and Vikingsholm

Drive Highway 89 north to Emerald Bay. Vikingsholm sits at the water at the end of a steep 1-mile trail from the lot on Highway 89; a gentler 1.5-mile trail starts at Eagle Point Campground. [T4] Parking is about $10 per vehicle per day or $3 per hour, sunrise to sunset, and the lot fills and can close in peak season; then park at Eagle Falls or D.L. Bliss. [T4] House tours ran daily from May 23 to September 30 in 2026, every half hour from 10:30 am to 4 pm, first come, first served, about $18 for adults. [T4] Dogs aren't allowed on the Vikingsholm trails. [T4]

> **Did you know?** Lora Knight bought the bay in 1928 and built the 38-room Scandinavian-style house in 1929 with about 200 workers. [T4] She also had a small stone teahouse built on Fannette Island, the lake's only island. [T4]

In winter, this stretch of Highway 89 can close after storms; see the winter box in Practical Matters. [T5]

## D.L. Bliss State Park and the Rubicon Trail

Just north of Emerald Bay, D.L. Bliss State Park reopened for camping and day use on May 21, 2026. [T6] Day use runs sunrise to sunset, about $10 per vehicle, and from October 1 the park is open to walkers only. [T6] The Rubicon Trail follows the shore toward Emerald Bay; dogs aren't allowed on the beach or trails. [T6]

## Where to stay

- **Basecamp Hotel** (budget, $): stylish, two blocks from Heavenly Village. [STAY-SOUTH]
- **The Coachman Hotel** (mid, $$): renovated in 2021. [STAY-SOUTH]
- **Camp Richardson Historic Resort** (mid, $–$$): hotel, cabins and a beach on the lake between town and Emerald Bay. [STAY-SOUTH]
- **Edgewood Tahoe Resort**, Stateline (upper, $$$): lakefront, with a golf course; resort fee. [STAY-SOUTH]

## Where to eat

- **Ernie's Coffee Shop** ($): breakfast and lunch since 1968. [EAT-SOUTH]

Prices, hours and seasons change, so check before you go.
