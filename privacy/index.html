Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

thetripnow.com is run by PTK Studio. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

If you choose Allow in the banner, we count visits with Google Analytics 4 (via Google Firebase): which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from (country, referring site) and the device type. This helps us see what people find useful. If you choose No thanks, or your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

Your choice is stored only in your browser. You can change it at any time: .

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Links to other sites

Links on this site (for example to stores, YouTube or Amazon) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Feedback

If you send us feedback in The Trip Now (through the feedback button, form or app screen), we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, an optional star rating, the page or app version you were using, and your language setting. We ask for your email only if you want a reply, and we keep it only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you; we delete it after at most 90 days. We don't use cookies or tracking for feedback, and we don't collect device identifiers. We also store the IP address your feedback was sent from, to prevent abuse; it is deleted with your feedback after one year. Feedback is stored by PTK Studio's 88eggs service (hosted in the United States), used only to fix problems and improve The Trip Now, never sold or shared, and deleted after one year. To have your feedback deleted sooner, contact us at hello@ptk-studio.com.
